## Domain rationale
Algebra is the central tool for describing and analyzing relationships. Students move from specific numerical reasoning to abstract general thinking.

**Goals:**
- Develop symbolic thinking; understand variables as general representatives
- Free movement between representations: verbal ↔ algebraic ↔ table ↔ graph
- Understand functional relationships
- Develop skills for solving real-world problems

## Guiding principles
1. **Concrete to abstract:** start from familiar situations
2. **Multiple representations:** verbal ↔ algebraic ↔ table ↔ graph
3. **Generalization:** from specific cases to general rules
4. **Spiral:** each grade adds depth

## Balance: algorithmic vs strategic
- Algorithmic: collecting like terms; solving equations
- Strategic: translate language; visualize; use tools; explain process
