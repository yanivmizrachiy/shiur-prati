## Purpose
Single-page official scheduling document. Shows yearly teaching sequence for grade 8, 2026–2027, with hours per topic per domain.

---

## Period 1: Until mid-January

| Domain | Topics | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Numeric | Ratio, proportion, scale, percentages | ~15 |
| Geometric | Geometric completions | ~6 |
| Uncertainty | Data collection, relative frequency | ~8 |

---

## Mid-year (after mid-January)

| Domain | Topics | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Geometric | Shapes and transformations | ~6 |
| Algebra | Introduction to functions; multiple representations | ~8 |
| Uncertainty | Spread measures; two information sources | ~12 |
| Algebra cont. | Linear graphs; slope; interpolation; equations with motion | ~19 |

---

## Period 2: From mid-January

| Domain | Topics | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Algebra | Word problems; systems of equations basic | ~8 |
| Geometry | Triangle congruence; SSS/SAS/ASA; similarity | ~28 |
| Uncertainty | Probability | ~6 |
| Algebra final | Inequalities; systems continued; word problems | ~10 |
| Geometry end | Pythagoras in 3D space | ~6 |

## Key observations for curriculum map
1. Grade 8 spans all four domains in tightly sequenced schedule
2. Geometry dominates second half — 10+ weeks on triangles
3. Algebra grows throughout: functions → linear → systems
4. Uncertainty distributed across multiple blocks
5. Pythagoras appears twice: numeric context (grade 7 carry-over) and 3D (end of year)
6. No grade 9 content in this document
