## Domain rationale
Geometry develops spatial thinking, visualization, and formal/informal justification. Integrates visual reasoning with logical justification.

---

## Interdomain connections

### Algebra ↔ Geometry
- Algebraic expressions for areas: a×b, a×b×c
- Variables in geometric formulas: r in 2πr, h in triangle
- Equations in geometric context: find side given area
- Pythagoras as algebraic relation: a²+b²=c²

### Numeric ↔ Geometry
- Areas with fractional measurements
- Powers and roots: areas (m²), Pythagoras (√)
- Ratio/proportion: scale, similarity
- Negative numbers: coordinate system (all four quadrants)

## Guiding principles
1. **Physical to abstract:** start with paper folding, cutting, measuring
2. **Visualization:** mental rotation; 2D↔3D transitions
3. **Proof and justification:** gradual from informal to formal (formal only in grade 9)
4. **Connections:** 2D↔3D; geometry↔algebra; between shapes
